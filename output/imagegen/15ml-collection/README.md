# The 15 ml collection — final gallery images

Generated using the built-in imagegen tool. Each fragrance has three final images:

- `*-15ml-bottle.png`: main bottle photograph.
- `*-15ml-scene.png`: elevated alternate angle.
- `*-15ml-detail.png`: close-up of gold lettering, glass and naturally settled gold leaf.

The three fragrance names are `amber-hour`, `open-sky` and `iron-crown`. These nine final PNGs are the source originals. Optimized WebP copies are installed in `public/media/` under the same names.

The final bottle has a small fitted protective cap, glossy polished-gold cap and atomizer collar, small lateral gold AFTER BELLS branding based on the supplied vector wordmark, a smaller parallel fragrance name, a clear dip tube and minute gold leaf flakes concentrated naturally near the base. Gold lettering occupies about one-fifth of the glass body's length. Liquid colours are warm honey amber, very pale straw and deep cognac amber respectively.
